Results obtained with “Calculate_connectivity_v08.R”, with PLD calculated using the equation from O'Connor et al. (2007), in which the species-specific intercept β0i was calibrated as indicated by the authors.

Temperature used to calculate PLD was the mean temperature along larval dispersal trajectories (considering all larvae, including those not releasable). By adding 3 days to the PLD, I obtain β0 = 3.310.

There are only two future scenarios: fixed spawning or variable spawning.
